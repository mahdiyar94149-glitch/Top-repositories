How Direct Telegram Concierge Ordering Works

Rather than automated mass-checkout queues, Maison Vespera pairs every collector with an assigned Geneva specialist via Telegram for edition number selection, bespoke caseback engraving, and insured courier routing.

01. Curate Your Allocation Bag

Select your preferred timepieces, travertine luminaires, or extraits de parfum. Your selection persists automatically across all pages.

02. Enter Dispatch & Engraving Coordinates

Open the Telegram Bag drawer and specify your name, Telegram handle, destination city, and preferred settlement method (Bank Wire, USDT, or Private Invoice).

03. One-Click Encrypted Telegram Dispatch

Launch the pre-formatted order dossier directly into Telegram (`@MaisonVesperaConcierge`) or copy the reference code for immediate verification.
