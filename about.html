Material Provenance & Disciplines

Three Pillars of the Maison

Select a discipline below to inspect our raw material sourcing, tolerances, and finishing standards.

Geneva Calibre Workshop

Free-Sprung Architecture & Hand Anglage

Every Maison Vespera watch case is CNC-milled from medical-grade Ti-6Al-4V bar stock and finished with alternating satin-brushed flanks and mirror-polished chamfers. Inside, our column-wheel and openworked calibres employ beryllium-bronze balance wheels for thermal stability.

Acoustic & Isochronism Testing

Each assembled watch undergoes 14 days of five-position thermal cycling between 8°C and 38°C before receiving its individual timing dossier.

Via Margutta Lapidary Studio

Unfilled Roman Travertine & Patinated Brass

We source raw sedimentary stone blocks from family-operated quarries in Tivoli outside Rome. We never fill the natural mineral pores with synthetic resin—preserving the tactile cavities formed by thermal springs over millennia.

Dry-Fit Architectural Joinery

Perforated aged brass diffusers and collars are mechanically keyed into the stone without chemical adhesives, allowing lifetime servicing.

Grasse & Florence Workshops

Six-Month Stone Maceration & Vegetable Cordovan

Our extraits are compounded at 32% to 35% oil concentration using wild-harvested Omani frankincense, roasted Java vetiver, and supercritical CO₂ labdanum extracts, bottled in heavy smoked glass with solid turned brass caps.

Saddle-Stitched Shell Cordovan

Our architectural document briefs are stitched entirely by hand using waxed linen thread and fitted with sand-cast solid brass hardware.
